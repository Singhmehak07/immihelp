## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Lower Back Pain (Muscle Strain)
**Symptoms:** dull aching pain in lower back, stiffness, muscle spasms, pain worsens with bending or lifting, limited range of motion  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Apply ice pack for first 48 hours (20 min on, 20 min off), then switch to warm compress  
2. Take Paracetamol 500mg every 6 hours or Ibuprofen 400mg with food every 8 hours  
3. Gentle stretching — cat-cow stretches, knee-to-chest pulls, pelvic tilts  
4. Stay moderately active — avoid prolonged bed rest beyond 1–2 days  
5. Sleep with a pillow between the knees (side sleepers) or under the knees (back sleepers)  

**When to seek help:** pain radiates down legs, numbness or tingling, loss of bladder/bowel control, pain after a fall or injury, lasts >6 weeks  

**DO NOT:** lift heavy objects, sit for prolonged periods without breaks, twist your spine forcefully  

**Escalation_Required:** FALSE

## RISK_LEVEL: HIGH
### Condition: Sciatica (Nerve Compression)
**Symptoms:** sharp shooting pain from lower back down through the buttock and leg, numbness or tingling in leg/foot, weakness in affected leg, pain worsens with sitting  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Apply alternating ice and heat to the lower back  
2. Take NSAIDs like Ibuprofen 400mg with food for pain and inflammation  
3. Perform gentle piriformis stretches and nerve gliding exercises  
4. Avoid prolonged sitting — stand and walk every 30 minutes  
5. Use a firm mattress and maintain good posture  

**When to seek help:** progressive weakness in leg, loss of bladder or bowel control (cauda equina — emergency), bilateral leg symptoms, severe unrelenting pain  

**DO NOT:** do heavy lifting, prolonged forward bending, or high-impact exercise  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE
