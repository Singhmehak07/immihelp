## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Sore Throat & Mild Cough (Viral Pharyngitis)
**Symptoms:** scratchy/dry throat, mild pain while swallowing, dry cough, no fever  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Gargle with warm salt water  
2. Take 1–2 teaspoons of honey  
3. Do steam inhalation  

**When to seek help:** lasts >7 days, severe pain while swallowing, high fever  

**DO NOT:** give honey to infants under 1 year  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Mild Nausea & Indigestion
**Symptoms:** queasiness, mild stomach ache, bloating, motion sickness  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Consume ginger (raw or tea)  
2. Drink peppermint tea  
3. Use lemon/aroma for relief  

**When to seek help:** vomiting >24 hours, cannot hold water, severe localized pain  

**DO NOT:** lie flat after eating or force heavy food  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

### Condition: Mild Insomnia
