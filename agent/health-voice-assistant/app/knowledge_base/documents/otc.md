## RISK_LEVEL: LOW_TO_MODERATE
### Condition: General Pain, Fever & Inflammation
**Symptoms:** headache, fever, muscle aches, joint pain, cramps, back pain  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Take Paracetamol (Acetaminophen) within safe limits  
2. Use NSAIDs like Ibuprofen/Naproxen (with food)  
3. Apply hot/cold compress  

**When to seek help:** fever >39.4°C, lasts >3 days, severe or persistent pain  

**DO NOT:** exceed max dose or mix multiple paracetamol medicines  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Upper Respiratory Infection (Cold, Cough)
**Symptoms:** cough, mucus, nasal congestion, sinus pressure  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Use cough suppressants (dry cough)  
2. Use expectorants (wet cough)  
3. Use decongestants or saline spray  
4. Rest and hydrate  

**When to seek help:** chest pain, breathlessness, blood in mucus  

**DO NOT:** use cough suppressant for wet cough  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE  

---

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW_TO_MODERATE
### Condition: Allergies & Hypersensitivity
**Symptoms:** runny nose, sneezing, itchy skin, watery eyes, hives  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Take antihistamines (Cetirizine, Loratadine, etc.)  
2. Use Benadryl for severe cases (night)  

**When to seek help:** swelling of lips/tongue, breathing difficulty  

**DO NOT:** drive after sedating antihistamines  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW_TO_MODERATE
### Condition: Gastrointestinal Issues
**Symptoms:** heartburn, diarrhea, constipation, bloating, nausea  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Use antacids or PPIs for acidity  
2. Use ORS and anti-diarrheal meds  
3. Use laxatives for constipation  
4. Stay hydrated  

**When to seek help:** blood in stool, severe pain, dehydration  

**DO NOT:** rely on laxatives long-term  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Skin Issues & Minor Injuries
**Symptoms:** rashes, fungal infection, cuts, burns, bites  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Use hydrocortisone for rashes  
2. Apply antifungal creams  
3. Clean wounds and apply antibiotic ointment  

**When to seek help:** infection signs, deep wounds, severe burns  

**DO NOT:** stop antifungal treatment early  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE  

---

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Eye, Ear & Minor Issues
**Symptoms:** dry eyes, irritation, earwax, warts  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Use artificial tears  
2. Use antihistamine eye drops  
3. Use ear drops for wax  
4. Apply salicylic acid for warts  

**When to seek help:** vision loss, severe pain, ear discharge  

**DO NOT:** insert objects into ear  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE
