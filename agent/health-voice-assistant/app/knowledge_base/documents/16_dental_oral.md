## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Toothache
**Symptoms:** sharp, throbbing, or constant tooth pain, swelling around the tooth, pain when chewing or biting, sensitivity to hot and cold, bad taste in mouth, headache  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Rinse mouth with warm salt water (1/2 teaspoon salt in 1 cup warm water)  
2. Take Ibuprofen 400mg with food every 8 hours (most effective for dental pain)  
3. Apply clove oil (eugenol) to the affected tooth using a cotton ball — natural antiseptic and numbing agent  
4. Use an OTC numbing gel (benzocaine-based) on the gum around the painful tooth  
5. Apply a cold compress to the outside of the cheek for 20 minutes on/off  
6. Floss gently around the tooth to remove any trapped food  

**When to seek help:** fever, swelling that spreads to the face or jaw, difficulty breathing or swallowing, pus discharge, pain persists >2 days despite pain relief, broken or knocked-out tooth  

**DO NOT:** apply aspirin directly on the gum (causes burns), use very hot or cold food/drink on the affected side, ignore persistent dental pain (may indicate abscess)  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Mouth Ulcers (Canker Sores / Aphthous Ulcers)
**Symptoms:** small, round or oval sores inside the mouth, white or yellow center with red border, painful especially when eating or talking, usually on inner lips, cheeks, tongue, or soft palate  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Apply OTC mouth ulcer gel (with benzocaine or choline salicylate) for pain relief  
2. Rinse mouth with warm salt water 2–3 times daily  
3. Use an antimicrobial mouthwash (chlorhexidine-based) to prevent secondary infection  
4. Apply a paste of baking soda and water directly to the ulcer  
5. Eat soft, bland foods — avoid spicy, acidic, or rough-textured foods  
6. Use a soft toothbrush to avoid irritating the ulcer  
7. Ensure adequate intake of Vitamin B12, Iron, and Folate (deficiency can cause recurrent ulcers)  

**When to seek help:** ulcers last >3 weeks, unusually large ulcers, painless ulcers (may indicate something more serious), frequent recurrences (>4 times/year), difficulty eating or drinking, fever accompanies ulcers  

**DO NOT:** use toothpaste containing sodium lauryl sulfate (SLS) if prone to ulcers, eat crunchy or sharp foods that may injure the mouth lining, bite cheeks or lips  

**Escalation_Required:** FALSE

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Bleeding Gums (Gingivitis)
**Symptoms:** gums that bleed during brushing or flossing, red swollen tender gums, bad breath (halitosis), receding gum line, soft puffy gums  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Brush teeth twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush using gentle circular motions  
2. Floss daily to remove plaque between teeth  
3. Use an antiseptic mouthwash (chlorhexidine or cetylpyridinium chloride)  
4. Rinse with warm salt water to reduce inflammation  
5. Eat vitamin C-rich foods (oranges, strawberries, bell peppers) — deficiency worsens gum health  
6. Avoid tobacco products — major risk factor for gum disease  
7. Replace toothbrush every 3 months  

**When to seek help:** bleeding does not stop, gums are severely swollen or painful, teeth feel loose, persistent bad breath despite good hygiene, gum recession exposing tooth roots  

**DO NOT:** brush too hard or use a hard-bristled toothbrush, ignore bleeding gums (early sign of gum disease), skip dental check-ups  

**Escalation_Required:** FALSE
