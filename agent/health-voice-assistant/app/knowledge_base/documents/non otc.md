## RISK_LEVEL: HIGH
### Condition: Malaria
**Symptoms:** sudden high fever, chills, shivering, sweating, headache, vomiting  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Bed rest under mosquito net  
2. Tepid sponging for fever  
3. Maintain hydration  

**When to seek help:** fever >48 hours or convulsions  

**DO NOT:** use NSAIDs like ibuprofen/aspirin without diagnosis  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: CRITICAL
### Condition: Dengue Fever
**Symptoms:** high fever, severe joint/muscle pain, pain behind eyes, rash, bleeding signs  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Absolute bed rest  
2. Use paracetamol for fever  
3. Maintain high fluid intake  

**When to seek help:** vomiting, abdominal pain, bleeding  

**DO NOT:** take aspirin or diclofenac  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE  

---

## RISK_LEVEL: HIGH
### Condition: Tuberculosis (TB)
**Symptoms:** cough >2 weeks, blood in sputum, night sweats, weight loss, fever  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Isolate patient in ventilated room  
2. Use mask  
3. Maintain hygiene  

**When to seek help:** immediately if symptoms persist >2 weeks  

**DO NOT:** stop medication early  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE  

---

## RISK_LEVEL: CRITICAL
### Condition: Cholera
**Symptoms:** severe diarrhea, vomiting, dehydration, leg cramps  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Continuous ORS intake  
2. Keep patient cool  
3. Replace fluids rapidly  

**When to seek help:** no urine >6 hours or cannot retain fluids  

**DO NOT:** give anti-diarrheal drugs  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE  

---

## RISK_LEVEL: CRITICAL
### Condition: Pneumonia
**Symptoms:** fast breathing, chest in-drawing, fever, bluish lips  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Keep patient upright  
2. Provide warm fluids  
3. Use steam inhalation  

**When to seek help:** breathing difficulty or rapid breathing  

**DO NOT:** expose to smoke  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: HIGH
### Condition: Hypertension
**Symptoms:** often none, severe cases include headache, nosebleeds  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Rest during spikes  
2. Daily exercise/yoga  
3. Monitor BP  

**When to seek help:** BP >180/120 or chest pain  

**DO NOT:** consume high-salt foods  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: HIGH
### Condition: Type 2 Diabetes
**Symptoms:** frequent urination, thirst, slow healing, blurred vision  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Monitor blood sugar  
2. Inspect feet daily  
3. Control diet  

**When to seek help:** wounds not healing  

**DO NOT:** walk barefoot  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: CRITICAL
### Condition: Heart Attack
**Symptoms:** chest pain radiating to arm/jaw, sweating  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Sit patient down  
2. Loosen clothing  

**When to seek help:** immediately  

**DO NOT:** allow movement  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: CRITICAL
### Condition: Stroke
**Symptoms:** facial droop, arm weakness, slurred speech  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Lay patient with head elevated  
2. Note time of onset  

**When to seek help:** immediately  

**DO NOT:** give food or water  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: CRITICAL
### Condition: Snakebite
**Symptoms:** swelling, paralysis, breathing difficulty  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Keep patient still  
2. Immobilize limb  

**When to seek help:** immediately  

**DO NOT:** cut wound or suck venom  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE
