## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye)
**Symptoms:** redness in the white of the eye, increased tearing, thick yellow-green discharge (bacterial), watery discharge (viral), itchy eyes (allergic), gritty feeling, crusting on eyelids especially upon waking, sensitivity to light  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Clean eyelids gently with a warm, damp cloth to remove crusting  
2. Apply warm compress to the closed eye for 5–10 minutes, several times daily  
3. Use artificial tears (preservative-free) to soothe irritation  
4. For allergic conjunctivitis: use antihistamine eye drops (Ketotifen)  
5. Wash hands frequently to prevent spreading  
6. Do not share towels, pillowcases, or eye makeup  
7. Discard eye makeup used during the infection  
8. Avoid wearing contact lenses until fully recovered  

**When to seek help:** severe eye pain, significant vision changes, sensitivity to light, newborn with eye discharge (ophthalmia neonatorum — emergency), symptoms worsen after 3 days, thick green discharge that returns throughout the day  

**DO NOT:** rub your eyes, share personal items, wear contact lenses during infection, use steroid eye drops without prescription  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Digital Eye Strain (Computer Vision Syndrome)
**Symptoms:** tired or sore eyes, dry eyes or excessive tearing, blurred or double vision, headache, neck and shoulder pain, difficulty focusing, increased sensitivity to light, difficulty keeping eyes open  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds  
2. Blink frequently and deliberately (we blink less when staring at screens)  
3. Use artificial tears to combat dryness  
4. Position screen at arm's length, top of screen at or slightly below eye level  
5. Reduce screen glare with an anti-glare screen filter or adjust room lighting  
6. Increase text size on devices for comfortable reading  
7. Take a 15-minute break from screens every 2 hours  
8. Use night mode or blue-light filtering glasses in the evening  

**When to seek help:** persistent vision changes even after screen breaks, severe headaches, double vision that does not resolve, eye pain  

**DO NOT:** stare at screens for hours without breaks, use devices in a dark room (causes extra eye strain), ignore persistent symptoms  

**Escalation_Required:** FALSE
