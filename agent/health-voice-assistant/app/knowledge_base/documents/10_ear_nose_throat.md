## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Ear Infection (Otitis Media) — Mild
**Symptoms:** ear pain, feeling of fullness in the ear, mild hearing reduction, mild fever, irritability in children, fluid drainage from ear  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Apply a warm compress to the affected ear for 15–20 minutes  
2. Take Paracetamol or Ibuprofen for pain and fever  
3. Use over-the-counter ear drops (analgesic type) for pain relief  
4. Elevate the head while sleeping to promote drainage  
5. Stay hydrated and rest  

**When to seek help:** severe ear pain, high fever >39°C, pus or blood draining from ear, hearing loss persists, symptoms in infant <6 months, symptoms worsen after 48–72 hours  

**DO NOT:** insert cotton buds or objects into the ear, swim until fully recovered, use ear drops if eardrum may be perforated  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Sinusitis (Sinus Infection)
**Symptoms:** facial pain and pressure around eyes/cheeks/forehead, thick yellow-green nasal discharge, nasal congestion, reduced sense of smell, headache, post-nasal drip, cough (worse at night)  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Do steam inhalation 2–3 times daily (add eucalyptus oil for extra benefit)  
2. Use saline nasal spray or neti pot to clear passages  
3. Apply warm compress over the sinuses  
4. Take Paracetamol or Ibuprofen for pain and pressure  
5. Use decongestant nasal spray (max 3 days to avoid rebound congestion)  
6. Stay hydrated — warm fluids help thin mucus  

**When to seek help:** symptoms persist >10 days, high fever, severe headache not relieved by painkillers, visual disturbances, swelling around the eyes, recurrent sinusitis (>4 episodes/year)  

**DO NOT:** use decongestant sprays for more than 3 consecutive days, fly during acute sinusitis, smoke or expose to secondhand smoke  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE

## RISK_LEVEL: LOW
### Condition: Tonsillitis
**Symptoms:** sore throat, difficulty swallowing, swollen red tonsils (may have white patches), fever, bad breath, neck tenderness, headache  

**Home Care Advice:**
1. Gargle with warm salt water (1/2 teaspoon salt in 1 cup warm water) several times daily  
2. Take Paracetamol or Ibuprofen for pain and fever  
3. Drink warm liquids — tea with honey, broth, warm water with lemon  
4. Eat soft foods — yogurt, mashed potatoes, smoothies  
5. Use throat lozenges or anesthetic sprays for temporary relief  
6. Rest your voice and get plenty of sleep  

**When to seek help:** difficulty breathing, inability to swallow fluids, drooling, muffled voice (peritonsillar abscess), symptoms persist >5 days, recurrent tonsillitis (>5 episodes/year)  

**DO NOT:** give honey to children under 1 year, give aspirin to children under 16, smoke or drink alcohol  

**Escalation_Required:** TRUE
